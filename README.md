# YouTube Distraction & Algorithm Blocker

A lightweight custom filter list for **AdGuard**, **uBlock Origin**, and **Brave Browser** designed to strip away YouTube's algorithmic hooks, endless feeds, and distractions, helping you stay focused.

## 🚫 What It Blocks

* **Home Feed:** Disables the algorithmic homepage loop entirely, leaving a clean, blank screen so you only watch what you intentionally search for.
* **Sidebar Recommendations:** Removes the "Up Next" column next to the video player to prevent infinite clicking.
* **YouTube Shorts:** Hides Shorts shelves from appearing in your search results and sidebars.
* **End Screen Suggestions:** Blocks grid popups and video recommendations that overlap the screen when a video finishes playing.

##  Alternate Installation Methods

### Method 2: Manual URL Import
If the one-click button does not launch your blocker automatically, you can subscribe via the raw URL:
1. Copy this link: `https://githubusercontent.com`
2. Open your blocker's settings (**AdGuard** > **Filters** > **Custom** OR **uBlock Origin** > **Filter lists** > **Import**).
3. Paste the URL and click **Subscribe** or **Apply changes**.

### Method 3: Brave Browser Settings
1. Copy the raw link provided in Method 2.
2. Navigate to `brave://settings/shields/filters` in your address bar.
3. Scroll down to **Add custom filter lists**, paste the URL, and click **Add**.

---

##  Mobile Platform Limitations
Please note that network extensions like AdGuard cannot inject custom cosmetic hiding filters directly into the **official YouTube Android or iOS apps** due to operating system sandbox restrictions. 

To use this blocklist on a mobile device, you must watch YouTube through a privacy-focused mobile browser equipped with your content blocker (e.g., Safari with AdGuard on iOS, or Firefox with uBlock Origin on Android).
