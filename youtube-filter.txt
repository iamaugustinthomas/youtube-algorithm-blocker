! Title: YouTube Distraction & Algorithm Blocker
! Description: Blocks the YouTube home feed, sidebar recommendations, Shorts, and end screens to prevent algorithmic rabbit holes.
! Homepage: https://github.com
! Expires: 7 days
! Version: 1.0.0

! ----------------------------------------------------------------
! 1. HOME FEED BLOCKER
! ----------------------------------------------------------------
! Completely hides the YouTube Home Feed (leaves it blank)
youtube.com###page-manager > ytd-browse[page-type="home"]

! ----------------------------------------------------------------
! 2. SIDEBAR RECOMMENDATIONS
! ----------------------------------------------------------------
! Removes the "Up Next" sidebar when watching videos
youtube.com##ytd-watch-flexy #secondary

! ----------------------------------------------------------------
! 3. SHORTS REMOVAL
! ----------------------------------------------------------------
! Hides the Shorts rows from search results and feeds
youtube.com##ytd-reel-shelf-renderer
youtube.com##ytd-rich-shelf-renderer[is-shorts]

! ----------------------------------------------------------------
! 4. END SCREEN ELEMENTS
! ----------------------------------------------------------------
! Hides the tile video recommendations that appear over the player at the end of a video
youtube.com##.html5-endscreen
youtube.com##.ytp-ce-element
